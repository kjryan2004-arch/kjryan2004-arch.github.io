Our promise.

Your event, your details, your name on it. PostCheck keeps them straight and leaves the decisions to you.

  • It asks before it assumes.Anything the flyer doesn’t say is asked about or clearly marked, never invented.
  • It shows its work.Every field says where it came from: your flyer, your answer, or your saved details.
  • It waits for your OK.The post button stays off until every flagged detail has been checked.
  • It leaves approval to campus.Staff review every event in EngageXU, just as they do today.

What it won’t do.

  • Approve events.That stays with campus staff.
  • Read Canva links.Export your design as an image or PDF first.
  • Design your flyer.Bring the one you already made.
  • Book rooms or track RSVPs.It handles the post, and only the post.
  • Post anywhere else.No Instagram, no group chats.

Questions.

Do my events still need campus approval?

Yes. You still submit the event in EngageXU, and campus staff review it the same way they always have. PostCheck can’t post to EngageXU directly yet, so it gives you each field ready to paste. It also keeps a copy so you can start from it next time.

What can I start from?

A photo or screenshot of your flyer, an image or PDF, a few typed lines (day, time, place, food, who’s speaking), or an event you’ve posted before.

What if the flyer is missing something?

PostCheck asks you one short question at a time, such as “When does it end?” If you don’t know yet, skip it. The field stays marked until you fill it in.

Is PostCheck part of Xavier or EngageXU?

No. PostCheck is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with Xavier University or CampusGroups.

Who can join the pilot?

Student officers who post their organization’s events to EngageXU: presidents, events chairs, marketing chairs and anyone else who does the posting.
